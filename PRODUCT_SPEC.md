# Auction to Glory
A multiplayer cricket management game where squad investment affects probabilistic T20 results.

## Phase 1 acceptance
Original premium dark auction presentation, accessible responsive controls, fictional demonstration players, home and league lobby. An isolated local demo is playable through bidding, expiry, settlement and next nominations. Server validates bids, deadlines, purse, squad size and overseas caps. Demo opponents are explicitly simulated. No claim of connected multiplayer.

## Future product
Private rooms for 2–10 franchises, default ₹120 crore, configurable rules, persistent auctions, squad management, seeded matches, tactical decisions and leagues. Ratings and observed data remain separate. Authentication and durable transactional storage precede public multiplayer.
